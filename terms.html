Booking Terms

Effective date: 16 November 2026

These terms describe how quotation requests work for Manila Pampanga Go Taxi private rides and tours.

With-driver service only

Manila Pampanga Go Taxi offers Sedan, MPV/SUV/AUV and Van categories with a driver. We coordinate trips using owned and partner vehicles. Vehicle models shown on the website are examples; the assigned unit and capacity depend on availability and will be confirmed before the trip.

Requesting a quotation

Submitting the website form or sending a WhatsApp message is not a confirmed reservation. We review your route, date, passengers, luggage, vehicle preference and availability, then communicate the proposed fare and trip details. A booking is confirmed only after Manila Pampanga Go Taxi explicitly confirms acceptance with you.

Prices and inclusions

Published popular-route fares are indicative one-way estimates, not guaranteed prices for every pickup or destination. Your final quotation will specify the route, vehicle category and any applicable tolls, parking, waiting time, additional stops, special pickup requirements or other charges. Please review and agree to the full quotation before confirming.

Schedule and changes

Advance booking is recommended. Same-day and urgent trips depend on availability. Please notify us as early as possible if pickup details, passenger count, route, travel date or time change; a revised quote or different vehicle may be required.

Booking confirmation and payments

Requesting a quotation is free. You do not need to pay to submit an inquiry, and an inquiry is not a reservation.

Advance bookings: A down payment is required to secure an advance reservation. We will tell you the amount, accepted payment method and any remaining balance in your individual quotation or booking confirmation before you make a payment. Your advance booking is confirmed only after we acknowledge the agreed down payment and explicitly confirm the ride.

Same-day bookings: No down payment is required. Payment is made on the trip day, with the agreed amount and payment method confirmed directly with you. Same-day rides remain subject to vehicle and driver availability and our explicit confirmation.

Never send money based on an unverified message. Confirm the recipient and payment instructions with Manila Pampanga Go Taxi through the contact details shown on this website.

Cancellation and rescheduling

If you need to cancel or reschedule, contact us as soon as possible using the booking contact details. Changes may affect driver availability, pickup details, fare or vehicle assignment. We will explain any applicable cancellation charge, deposit treatment, refund eligibility and rescheduling conditions before you pay or confirm the booking. These may depend on your specific trip and will be stated in your written booking agreement. There is no automatic cancellation fee simply for requesting a free quotation.

Refunds or adjustments of any down payment are handled according to the conditions expressly agreed for that booking and any applicable consumer rights. Please request written clarification of your specific refund terms before paying; do not assume that every down payment is automatically refundable or non-refundable.

Passengers and luggage

Provide accurate passenger and luggage details so we can recommend a suitable vehicle. Actual capacity depends on the assigned unit and safe luggage placement. Special requests, including pets, child seats or oversized items, should be discussed before confirmation.

Delays and service changes

Travel time can be affected by traffic, weather, road restrictions and other conditions. If a vehicle or driver change becomes necessary, we will communicate the available options where reasonably possible.
